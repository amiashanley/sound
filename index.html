ABOUT

I'm a London-based sound recordist and editor working across feature films, shorts, corporate and culture, podcasts, and arts-based moving image. I have experience in a range of settings, from intimate dialogue to spontaneous action, and remote locations to studio environments.

Full kit list and rate card available on request.

I also work across sound art, research, and education. For artistic and academic projects, publications, and talks, see amiashanley.com.
